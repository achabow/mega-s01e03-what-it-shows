0002 · Duplicates require the same demonstrated cause

owner Andrzej Chabowski proposed reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

Context

Incident review shows that identical subjects can hide different bugs, and different subjects can share a cause. The old triage page was reviewed in June 2024 and is stale under the kb’s 180-day rule. Its live explorer already disagrees with its subject-only instructions.

Decision proposed to the support desk

Supersede decision 0001’s subject-only deduplication rule. Join each report to its tenant and inspect locale, form, channel, and the reproduced mechanism. Merge only when both reports have the same non-null cause. An unknown tenant or an unexplained symptom needs investigation. A subject match alone is insufficient, and a subject difference alone is insufficient to split reports.
